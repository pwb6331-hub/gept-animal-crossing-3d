### 第二部分：段落填空 (Questions 11-20，含新制句子填空)
**(Passage: 都市垂直水耕農場的綠色革命)**
As the global population steadily surpasses eight billion, securing reliable food supplies while minimizing agricultural land degradation poses a monumental challenge. Traditional farming relies extensively on unpredictable rainfall, vast arable land, and synthetic pesticides. In recent years, high-tech vertical farming has emerged as a transformative alternative. **(11)** ________. Inside these climate-controlled facilities, leafy greens and strawberries are cultivated on stacked multi-tier trays utilizing nutrient-rich water solutions and customized LED lighting wavelengths. Because vertical farms operate indoors regardless of external weather extremes, crop yields remain consistent throughout the four seasons. **(12)** ________, closed-loop hydroponic systems recycle ninety-five percent of water, consuming far less resources than conventional open-field agriculture. By siting these facilities inside city centers, food miles and carbon emissions associated with long-distance freight shipping are drastically curtailed.

- **第 11 題 (新制句子選填)**:
  - (A) By growing crops vertically in controlled indoor spaces, this method maximizes harvest yield per square foot.
  - (B) Heavy snowstorms often cause railway power lines to freeze in alpine regions.
  - (C) Many passengers prefer buying first-class train tickets during holiday travels.
  - (D) Several department stores announced end-of-season clothing discount promotions.
  - **答案**: **(A)**
  - **詳解**: 前文提及垂直農場是突破性的替代方案，(A)「透過在受控室內空間垂直種植作物，該方法大幅提升了每平方英尺的收成產量」承前啟後。
- **第 12 題 (轉折詞填空)**:
  - (A) Furthermore
  - (B) On the contrary
  - (C) In contrast
  - (D) Unfortunately
  - **答案**: **(A)**
  - **詳解**: 前句講產量不受天候影響，後句進一步說明還能省水 95%，屬於遞進關係，選 (A) Furthermore (此外)。
